Our Story

Craftsmanship Passed Down Through Generations

Alberto Watch began as a small workshop with one simple belief: a watch should be more than a way to tell time. It should be a companion for life.

Today, every piece in our collection is carefully selected for its movement, finish and character. We work with trusted Swiss-grade mechanisms and skilled artisans who treat every watch as a work of art.
